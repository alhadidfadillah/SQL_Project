# Sales Pipeline Analysis
## About Data
B2B sales pipeline data from a fictitious company that sells computer hardware, including information on accounts, products, sales teams, and sales opportunities.
## The Goals
Analyze the performance of a sales team's ability to win deals and earn revenue.
## The Objectives
1. Pipeline Metrics: Assess the overall sales pipeline by looking at opportunities by month, time to close, win rate, and product data.
2. Sales Agent Performance: Assess the performance of sales agents, their managers, and regional offices.
3. Product Analysis: Analyze the sales performance and quantity sold of the company's product portfolio.
4. Account Analysis: Analyze the company's accounts to get a better understanding of the team's customers.
