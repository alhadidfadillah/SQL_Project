# Sales Pipeline Analysis
## About Data
B2B sales pipeline data from a fictitious company that sells computer hardware, including information on accounts, products, sales teams, and sales opportunities.

## The Objectives
Analyze the performance of a sales team's ability to win deals and earn revenue.

## The Approaches
1. Pipeline Metrics: assess the overall sales pipeline by looking at opportunities by month, time to close, win rate, and product data.
2. Sales Agent Performance: assess the performance of sales agents, their managers, and regional offices.
3. Product Analysis: analyze the sales performance and quantity sold of the company's product portfolio.
4. Account Analysis: analyze the company's accounts to get a better understanding of the team's customers.

## Key Findings
- The most sales opportunities each month were in July, with a total of 796 deal counts.
- The won deals took 9 days longer than the lost deals. It tells us that the won deals might need some more advanced negotiations, so it took a longer time.
- The lost deal percentage was around 37%.
- MG Special is the product with the highest win rate, while GTK 500 is the product with the lowest win rate.
- The top performing sales agent is Hayden Neloms with a 70.39% win rate.
- The agent who generated the most total revenue is Darcel Schlecht with 1,153,214 total revenue.
- Cara Losch is the best manager with the highest win rate (64.43%).
- We can see that the product with the highest revenue is GTXPro, but it is not the most units sold. The most sold product is GTX Basic.
- GTK 500 has the highest average difference between sales price and close value (60.53). It means that GTK 500 has an average of 60% discount on its product.
- The GTX series has the most total revenue, while GTK series has the least total revenue.
- China has the lowest revenue, while the United States has the highest revenue.
- The company and their subsidiary that had the highest total revenue is Acme Corporation.

## Recommendation
- We know that the won deals took 9 days longer than the lost deals on average. This isn't inherently negative; it may indicate that complex, valuable deals require more negotiation. Analyze what top performers like Hayden Neloms (70.39% win rate) and Darcel Schlecht (top revenue) are doing during this extended period. Are they providing more technical data? Involving product specialists? Create a "Playbook for Complex Deals" based on their strategies and train the entire team.
- Address the GTK 500 discounting issue. Develop an upsell path from GTX Basic to GTXPro. Launch a targeted campaign in China.
- Create account blueprints based on Acme. Conduct a full product portfolio review. Formalize and standardize the winning sales process across all teams.
- Promote the MG Special. Have Cara Losch coach other managers. Analyze the playbook of top agents.
